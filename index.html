The story

It remembers
what you did

“Why did you leave me there?
I was so cold…” — Misha

Arthur, 34, archivist. One last inventory of Archive-404 — a drowned Soviet bunker where a psi-anomaly turns guilt into living hallucinations.

Years ago Arthur abandoned his little brother Misha in the dark. Now the dark wears Misha's face and calls itself The Fade — a shadow that patrols the corridors, stares from the black…and hunts.

Gather the 5 keepsakes of your brother before the lift doors close, speak the words you never said — and earn REDEMPTION. Fail, and the shadow drags you down the shaft.

It wears the dark like skin

The Descent

Under the hood

Engineered fear

👁️

LIVING MADNESS

Darkness, sprinting on empty stamina and the monster's touch feed insanity 0–100. Tunnel vision, shaking hands, whispers behind you.

🔦

PHYSICAL FLASHLIGHT

Lagging sway, dying-battery flicker, brightness as the only charge meter. Its cone is the only thing the Fade fears.

👹

STALKER AI

Patrol / Chase / Retreat with line-of-sight checks. Touch boils madness into a full-screen screamer.

🎞️

WGSL POST-FX

Custom render-graph shader: trembling film grain and a vignette that thickens with your madness.

🔊

SPATIAL AUDIO

Footsteps and whispers positioned in 3D space — behind you. Headphones strongly recommended.

🧩

PROCEDURAL EVERYTHING

Seeded mazes, generated textures, per-act decay. Save/load restores the exact same world.

Field manual

Survive

🩸 Darkness feeds madness — never let the torch die. Green batteries restore +40.

🔦 Hold light on the shadow — it cannot stand it, but it learns to wait.
🧸 All 5 keepsakes unlock the good ending. Check every dead end.
⏱️ Act 3: 180 seconds. Run. Do not stop. Do not look back.
